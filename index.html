HOW YOU PLAY

1 · TRADE

Every company trades against its own pool, so every buy pushes the price up and every sell pushes it down. News, earnings and the economy move what it's really worth.

2 · TAKE CONTROL

Own more of a company than anyone else and you run it. Pay yourself, pay dividends, buy back shares. Or found your own company and take it public.

3 · PLAY DIRTY

Spread a rumor, file a frivolous lawsuit, pump your own stock. Lawyer up first. Get caught and you're fined, frozen and under investigation.

4 · WIN THE WEEK

The board ranks how much your net worth grew this week. The top ten split the weekly pot.
